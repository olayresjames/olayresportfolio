AgapAI

A connected mobile platform for emergency rescue, proactive health monitoring, and community support for senior citizens in Valenzuela City.

Live demo ↗ Source code ↗

My role

Product designer and full-stack developer. I shaped the product direction, interface, and connected user flows across the mobile and admin experiences.

The problem

Senior citizens, guardians, and local responders needed a clearer way to coordinate urgent help and ongoing health support.

What I built

React Native and Expo mobile flows, Firebase-backed real-time data, live SOS dispatching, health monitoring, and a Gemini-powered assistant.

Result

One centralized experience connects three audiences in a single operational loop, reducing the friction between an alert and the people who can respond.
